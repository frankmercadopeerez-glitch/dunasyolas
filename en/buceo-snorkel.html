Diving & Snorkeling: Underwater Adventure

Discover the underwater world of the Caribbean with guided diving or private snorkeling at the best reefs near Cartagena.

Home / Experiences / Diving & Snorkeling: Underwater Adventure
Direct accompaniment by Nohemi, backed by the agency's safety standards

Beneath the surface of the Caribbean, just off Cartagena, a world of coral reefs, tropical fish and crystal-clear waters awaits. The Corales del Rosario y San Bernardo National Natural Park is one of the most important marine ecosystems in Colombia, and the best way to experience it is by getting in the water.

Our Diving & Snorkeling experience offers two options: guided diving for all levels, including beginners, or private snorkeling straight to the best reefs, always with Nohemi coordinating your safety.

What's included?

Full gear Certified instructor Private snorkel guide Small or private groups

Where does it take place?

The activity takes place in the coral reefs of the Corales del Rosario y San Bernardo National Natural Park, sailing approximately one hour from Cartagena to selected spots based on sea conditions and the day's visibility.

Tip: if you've never dived before, don't worry. Our certified instructor explains everything before you get in the water and stays with you throughout the dive. Snorkeling is great for the whole family, including kids who can swim.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the Diving & Snorkeling tour?

It includes full diving or snorkeling gear, a certified instructor, a private snorkel guide and direct accompaniment by Nohemi or her team, with the agency's safety backup throughout the activity.

Do I need previous experience to go diving?

Not at all. Guided diving is designed for all levels, including beginners, since our certified instructor stays with you at all times.

Where does the diving and snorkeling take place?

The activity takes place in the coral reefs of the Corales del Rosario y San Bernardo National Natural Park, one of the most important marine ecosystems in the Colombian Caribbean.

Can I rent the whole boat for my group?

Yes. If you prefer full privacy, you can rent the entire boat starting at $3,200,000 COP (Interceptor 38', 16 pax) up to $3,900,000 COP (Virginia 420, 28 pax), great for groups, families or special events.
