Skydiving in Cartagena: Before You Book

Understand the tandem jump, the requirements and the day’s logistics.

What to know

The published tandem experience offers a jump from 10,000 feet with an instructor and Caribbean views, with photos and video included in the package. Confirm the exact meeting point, preparation time and operator requirements before paying.

Plan your visit

Skydiving is different from Paratrike: a Paratrike flight uses a powered wing and wheeled frame, while a skydive includes a parachute descent after leaving an aircraft. Tell the operator about relevant health or mobility concerns and ask whether the activity is suitable for you. Weather can affect the schedule.

Choose your experience

Compare the activities and day trips in our catalog, then send your date and group size to our team. We will confirm the itinerary, meeting point, price and conditions before you book.
