Where to Enjoy Sunset in Cartagena

Choose a waterfront viewpoint or a bay experience that fits your evening.

What to know

The city walls and waterfront offer different views from a boat in Cartagena Bay. Think about whether you want a relaxed walk, a seated experience or a cruise with music and drinks. Check opening hours and access conditions for any venue or monument you plan to enter.

Plan your visit

For a sunset cruise, confirm the departure pier, boarding time, duration and included services. Leave time to reach the pier before boarding and remember that clouds can change the view. City Bahía combines a city itinerary with a sunset boat experience; its details differ from the traditional city tour.
