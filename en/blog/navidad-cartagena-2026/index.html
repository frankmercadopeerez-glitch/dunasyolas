Combine festive city plans with a comfortable Caribbean holiday.

What to know

The announced December cultural program includes Christmas activities, novenas and the Pastel Cartagenero Festival. Check the official city agenda for venues, hours and updates rather than assuming that every event takes place daily. Official information.

Plan your visit

Book the experiences you most want early, then leave space for walks and family time. Ask restaurants and transport providers about holiday schedules. If you are traveling with children, balance crowded evening outings with quieter daytime plans and agree on an easy meeting point.
