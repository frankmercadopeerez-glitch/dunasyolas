Organize your arrival, documents and experiences before traveling.

What to know

Check current entry requirements with the official Colombian authorities and your airline for your nationality and travel documents. Review passport validity, onward travel and any required arrival forms before departure. Do not use an old social-media post as the final authority for your trip. Official information.

Plan your visit

For a short stay, separate your city day from your island day and allow time to arrive and settle in. Compare the total cost of transport, meals, excursions and optional services. Dunas & Olas can help coordinate experiences in Cartagena; your final itinerary and availability are confirmed before payment.
