What to know

Pico y placa restrictions depend on the vehicle, plate and applicable dates. Use the current municipal announcement linked below to check the rules for your specific vehicle and travel date; do not rely on a rotation from an earlier month. Official information.

Plan your visit

If you are renting a car, ask the rental company how restrictions affect your itinerary and what documentation you need. For a prearranged transfer, confirm the pickup time and meeting point. Keep extra time for city traffic when traveling to the airport, a pier or an excursion departure.
