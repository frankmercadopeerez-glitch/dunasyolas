Rain in Cartagena: What Happens to Your Excursion?

Plan flexibly and distinguish a rainy forecast from a departure decision.

What to know

Outdoor plans can change with weather, but rain alone does not tell you whether a specific activity will operate. Boat departures also depend on wind, waves and maritime restrictions. Ask your operator for the confirmed status rather than interpreting a forecast icon as a cancellation notice. Official information.

For a quick explanation of how rain can affect your plans, see Nohemi’s TikTok video. Watch Nohemi’s video on TikTok ↗

Plan your visit

Read the selected activity’s change and cancellation conditions before booking. Keep a city alternative and avoid tightly connecting an island return with a flight. For Paratrike, the published arrangement is to reschedule at no additional charge when the weather is unsuitable.
