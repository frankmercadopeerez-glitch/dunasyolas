Tour or day trip: what is the difference?

Understand what you are booking in Cartagena: the itinerary, time at each stop and what your chosen plan includes.

What is a tour?

A tour follows an itinerary, usually with guided visits or explanations. A Cartagena city tour, for example, visits historic landmarks and neighborhoods with a guide.

What is a day trip?

A day trip — called a pasadía in Spanish — gives you a day to enjoy a beach, island or beach club. Depending on the plan, it may include transport, lunch, activities and more than one stop. The name alone does not guarantee any particular inclusion.

Four and five islands

Our four- and five-island plans are listed as day trips. Each has its own itinerary and inclusions: read the individual plan before choosing. Visiting several islands does not mean spending the same amount of time at each one.

Before booking

  • Confirm the departure point, return time and time at each stop.
  • Check meals, drinks, transport, activities and any additional fees.
  • Tell us about children, accessibility needs or dietary requirements.
  • Confirm availability and the final price for your date.
