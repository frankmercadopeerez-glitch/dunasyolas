Choosing a Boat Trip to Cartagena’s Islands

Check the operator, departure arrangements and basic safety provisions.

What to know

Before boarding, confirm the boat and operator match your booking, ask about the authorized departure point and make sure a suitable life jacket is available for every passenger. Follow the crew’s instructions and do not accept overcrowding or pressure to ignore an official restriction. Official information.

Plan your visit

Ask about the route, planned return, weather procedure and included insurance. Keep the operator’s contact details and your booking confirmation. These checks help you ask informed questions; the decision to sail must follow the maritime authority’s current instructions and the operator’s safety procedures.
