What to know

October brings the Independence festivities’ preludes; the main Independence celebrations take place in November. December combines Christmas programming and the Pastel Cartagenero food festival. Check the city’s official agenda below before making arrangements. Official information.

Plan your visit

Choose your priorities before booking. On busy festival days, allow extra time for transfers and ask your accommodation about access to nearby streets. Keep an island day separate from an evening event if the return time is important to you.
