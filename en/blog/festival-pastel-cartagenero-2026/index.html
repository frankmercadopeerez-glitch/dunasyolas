Pastel Cartagenero Festival 2026

Discover a local food tradition during Cartagena’s December celebrations.

What to know

The city’s published cultural agenda schedules the Pastel Cartagenero Festival for December 17–26, 2026. Check the official program for the final venue, hours and participating cooks before heading out. Official information.

Plan your visit

Ask vendors about ingredients, portion sizes and prices before ordering, especially if you have allergies or dietary requirements. Plan time to enjoy the food without rushing to a transfer. You can combine your visit with a city walk, while keeping any separately booked activity’s meeting time in mind.
