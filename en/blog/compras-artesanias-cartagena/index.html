Shopping in Cartagena: Crafts and Souvenirs

Choose souvenirs with a clear origin and a price you understand.

What to know

Look for woven bags, hats, jewelry and locally made crafts that you genuinely want to use or keep. Ask who made the item, where it comes from and what materials it contains. Similar-looking pieces can differ considerably in workmanship and origin.

Plan your visit

Compare prices and agree on the total before buying. If an item is described as precious metal or a gemstone, request clear documentation instead of relying on a verbal promise. Check your airline’s baggage allowance and your destination’s customs rules for food, plants or other restricted goods.
