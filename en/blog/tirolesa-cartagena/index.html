Ziplining near Cartagena

Know what the five-zipline experience includes before you go.

What to know

The published experience includes five ziplines, safety equipment, traditional lunch and return transport, with an activity duration of approximately two hours. Confirm pickup, briefing time and the final itinerary with the operator.

Plan your visit

Ask about participant requirements, clothing and footwear when booking. Keep loose belongings secure and follow the instructor’s equipment and positioning instructions. Tell the team about relevant health or mobility concerns so the operator can assess suitability, and allow time for the journey as well as the activity.

Choose your experience

Compare the activities and day trips in our catalog, then send your date and group size to our team. We will confirm the itinerary, meeting point, price and conditions before you book.
