Before Sailing to Cartagena’s Islands

A short planning checklist for your island departure.

What to know

Confirm the provider, meeting point, departure time and boat arrangements before leaving for the pier. Keep your reservation available and arrive with enough time for boarding. Ask which port fees, park charges and insurance are included and which are paid separately. Official information.

Plan your visit

At boarding, follow the crew’s instructions, use the assigned life jacket and check the agreed return plan. If weather or an official restriction affects the trip, ask the operator about the next steps under your booking conditions. Never allow a tight schedule to pressure you into accepting an unsafe departure.
