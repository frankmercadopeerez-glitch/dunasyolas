Kitesurfing and Water Sports

Personally guided by Nohemi, with the agency's safety backing

The beaches north of Cartagena (La Boquilla) are one of the best kitesurfing spots in the Caribbean: steady winds and warm, flat waters, ideal for both beginners and advanced riders. We connect you with trusted certified schools and instructors.

Why do it with us:

We coordinate certified instructors, gear in great condition, and transport to the spot, so all you have to worry about is enjoying the Caribbean wind.
